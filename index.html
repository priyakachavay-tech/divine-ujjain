Mahakaleshwar Temple

Mahakaleshwar Temple is one of the twelve Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva, located in Ujjain, Madhya Pradesh. It is a famous pilgrimage destination known for its spiritual importance and the sacred Bhasma Aarti.

Harsidhdhi Temple

Harsiddhi Temple is one of the famous temples of Ujjain, dedicated to Goddess Harsiddhi. It is known for its spiritual significance and beautiful lamp pillars that look stunning when illuminated.

Bhairav Temple

Kal Bhairav Temple is a famous temple in Ujjain dedicated to Lord Kal Bhairav, a fierce form of Lord Shiva. The temple is known for its unique traditions and is an important spiritual destination for devotees.

Gadhkalika Temple

Gadkalika Temple is a renowned temple in Ujjain dedicated to Goddess Kalika. It is associated with the poet Kalidasa and attracts devotees seeking blessings, wisdom, and strength.

Ujjain is one of the oldest and holiest cities in India. It is famous for its temples, spiritual traditions, and rich cultural heritage.
